# Provenance

Everything in this repository — Game Forge (`game-forge/`), Atelier 3D (`shaddai-3d-atelier/`), the skills, guides and scripts — is SHADDAI-original work written from first principles and checked for text overlap against the third-party projects it was informed by (`node scripts/audit.mjs` runs the repository health check). No third-party source files, models or assets are bundled. General techniques (fixed timesteps, sparse sets, swept collision, spring cameras, projection maths, resampling kernels) are common engineering knowledge.

Model-weight and service licences (for anything you generate with Atelier 3D) are tracked per output in its provenance records.
